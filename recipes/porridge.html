Porridge

Ingredients

  • 1 Cup of rolled oats
  • 1.5 Cups of milk
  • ~70g Yoghurt
  • 5g Creatine
  • 30g Protein Powder
  • Spoonful Chia Seeds
  • Toppings of your choice (e.g., fruits, nuts, honey)

    • Instructions

    1. Combine oats and milk in a pot.
    2. Microwave for 3 minutes.
    3. Stir in yoghurt, creatine, protein powder, and chia seeds.
    4. Serve hot with your favorite toppings.
