Trifle

Ingredients

  • 1 packet of sponge fingers
  • 1 packet of jelly (flavor of your choice)
  • 1 cup of custard
  • 1 cup of whipped cream
  • Fresh fruits (e.g., strawberries, raspberries, blueberries)

Instructions

  1. Prepare the jelly according to the packet instructions and let it set in the fridge.
  2. In a trifle dish, layer the sponge fingers at the bottom.
  3. Pour the set jelly over the sponge fingers.
  4. Add a layer of custard on top of the jelly.
  5. Top with whipped cream and fresh fruits.
  6. Chill in the fridge for at least 2 hours before serving.
