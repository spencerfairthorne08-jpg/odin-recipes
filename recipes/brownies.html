Choch Fudgy 15 minute Brownies

These brownies are so easy to make and they are so delicious!

They are fudgy and chocolatey and will satisfy your sweet tooth in no time.

You can make them in just 15 minutes, so they are perfect for a quick dessert or snack.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup of sugar
  • 1/2 cup of flour
  • 1/3 cup of cocoa powder
  • 1/4 teaspoon of salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon of baking powder
  • 2 eggs
  • 1/2 cup of melted butter
  • 1 teaspoon of vanilla extract

Instructions

  1. Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C) and grease a 9x9 inch baking pan.
  2. In a medium bowl, mix together the sugar, flour, cocoa powder, salt, and baking powder.
  3. Add the eggs, melted butter, and vanilla extract to the dry ingredients and mix until well combined.
  4. Pour the batter into the prepared baking pan and spread it evenly.
  5. Bake for 20-25 minutes or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.
  6. Allow the brownies to cool in the pan before cutting them into squares and serving.
